Methodology & calculation rules

October 27, 2026 research snapshot from supplied Mission 1 files. JSON is the primary source; all 8 offer prices, representative flags and competitor identities were cross-checked with the workbook and report. No numerical conflicts were found.

Representative metrics use P01–P05: exactly one USD single-unit manual sorter per brand. Comparable product charts use those five plus IKEA P07. Bundles P06 and P08 remain in the register and are excluded from single-product statistics; P06 normalizes to $14.88 per cart but duplicates P01.

Explorer classifications use linear-interpolated representative quartiles: below minimum, at or below Q1 (budget), between Q1 and Q3 (market middle), at or above Q3 (premium), above maximum. “Comparable” means within ±5% of your selection; this is a display tolerance, not a demand estimate. All explorer comparisons use the full five-brand set, independent of profile filters.

Listed prices exclude tax, shipping and conditional discounts. Missing regular prices and availability remain unknown. Verification labels reproduce the supplied research and do not indicate a fresh website check.

Limits & open questions

    No direct automatic competitor was identified in this selective set; this does not prove none exists. Capacity, materials and features differ.

    Joseph Joseph's $182 set is marked promotional, but its advertised $196 worth is not an established historical regular price. Conditional checkout and card discounts are not applied.
